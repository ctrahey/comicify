## Canvas and composition

- {{ canvas.describe() }}.
- Fill the entire canvas (sky, ground, everything). No characters.
- Place the horizon as the set describes. Keep the lower third relatively

- Spread landmarks across the width so crops at different x positions each
  show something recognizable.
- Backgrounds may be more detailed than characters, but keep contrast
  moderate so characters and balloons stay readable on top.
