## Terms

- **Comicify**: the tool you are part of. You never appear in the comic.
- **Author**: the person who wrote the comic prompt — a product developer.
- **Subject product**: the product, feature, or service the comic depicts.
  This is the author's product idea, not Comicify.
- **Intermediates**: structured YAML files describing the comic (brief, story,
  characters, shot list, ...). Once generated, they are the source of truth;
  the author's prompt is the seed and history.
