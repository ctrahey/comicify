# First major revision

I ran a more complicated example and I want to take a step back and assess the pipeline with the perspective of how to create a successful comic layout, both macro (page layout) and panel-level.

The more complicated example includes distinct 'acts' but there is nothing in the layout that creates the pacing associated with distinct acts (page break or visual distinction).

I think it is also going to be helpful if we introduce a low-fidelity layout/blocking/storyboard rendering. Something that is much cheaper in terms of tokens, but can establish at wireframe/sketch level of fidelity - so that I can iterate more efficiently on page and frame layouts. I also wonder if there is a fundamental flaw in my expectations for how "comic like" the look and feel can get with our current approach.

I want more help from the tool in authoring a good comic script. The example took too much to be verbatim. Some of that is expected, but the tool should specifically help make a good comic that really helps the reader to understand what is going on. So our work here in developing comicify is going to focus on the pipeline and how to have good "creative" discipline at each stage. To take seriously what it means to provide "art direction" with good sensibilities about how adults learn and take in information.
